When reporting an error

Including the following helps us check it quickly:

  • Which calculator you used (salary, loan or bill split)
  • What you entered (for example: salary ₩50,000,000, meal allowance ₩200,000, 1 dependent)
  • The EasyCalc result and the value you expected, with its source if possible (a payslip or a bank repayment schedule)

To protect your privacy, please do not send sensitive information such as your resident registration number or bank account number.

Replies

We reply as soon as we have checked your message. Suggestions to improve our translations are welcome, in any of the languages we support.
