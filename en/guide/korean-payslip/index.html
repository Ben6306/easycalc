How to Read a Korean Payslip: A Guide for Foreign Workers

EasyCalc editors · 2026-10-18

If you work in Korea, your payslip (급여명세서, geuphyeo myeongseseo) is usually written only in Korean. This guide explains each line so you can check that your pay is correct, using a monthly salary of about ₩4.17 million as an example.

The two halves of a payslip

A Korean payslip has two parts. 지급 내역 (payments) lists everything your employer pays you. 공제 내역 (deductions) lists what is taken out before the money reaches your bank account. The amount you receive is 실수령액 (take-home pay), which is payments minus deductions.

Payments (지급 내역)

KoreanEnglishWhat it means
기본급Base payYour fixed monthly salary.
식대Meal allowanceUp to ₩200,000 a month is tax-free and is not counted for social insurance.
연장근로수당Overtime payPay for hours beyond the standard working hours.
야간근로수당Night work payExtra pay for work late at night.
휴일근로수당Holiday work payExtra pay for work on rest days or public holidays.
상여금BonusBonus payments, if your contract includes them.

Deductions (공제 내역)

KoreanEnglishYour share (2026)
국민연금National Pension4.75% of pay (capped at ₩6,590,000 of monthly pay)
건강보험Health Insurance3.595% of pay
장기요양보험Long-term Care Insurance13.14% of your health insurance amount
고용보험Employment Insurance0.9% of pay
소득세Income taxSet by the National Tax Service withholding table
지방소득세Local income tax10% of your income tax

The first four are called 4대보험, the "four major insurances". Your employer pays its own share of each on top of your salary. Industrial accident insurance (산재보험) is paid entirely by the employer and does not appear in your deductions.

Worked example

Annual salary ₩50,000,000, paid in 12 months, with a ₩200,000 tax-free meal allowance, single with no dependents:

Monthly gross pay₩4,166,667
National Pension− ₩188,380
Health Insurance− ₩142,600
Long-term Care Insurance− ₩18,730
Employment Insurance− ₩35,690
Income tax (withholding table)− ₩190,620
Local income tax− ₩19,060
Take-home payabout ₩3,571,587

About 14% of gross pay is deducted. Income tax comes from the official withholding table (effective March 1, 2026), the same table employers use, so it should match your payslip when the same pay and dependents are entered. The insurance lines can differ by a few won because of rounding.

Three things foreign workers often ask

Why is my income tax different every year in February?

Each February, employers run the year-end tax settlement (연말정산). If too much tax was withheld during the year, you get money back. If too little was withheld, extra tax is taken from your pay. This is normal.

Can I get my National Pension back when I leave Korea?

It depends on your nationality and Korea's agreement with your country. Many foreign workers can claim a lump-sum refund (반환일시금) when they leave Korea for good. Check with the National Pension Service before you leave, because the rules differ by country.

Do the rates change?

Yes. National Pension rises by 0.5 percentage points (total) each year from 2026 until it reaches 13% in 2033, and the health insurance rates are set again each year. The pension cap changes every July.

Check your own payslip

Enter your annual salary, meal allowance and number of dependents in the calculator to see the expected deduction for each line. If a line on your payslip is very different, ask your employer's payroll staff which items are included.
