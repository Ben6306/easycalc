This privacy policy explains how EasyCalc ("the site", easycalc.co.kr) handles information.

1. Information we collect

The site has no accounts and does not ask you for your name, contact details or other personal information.

Amounts, names and account notes you type into the calculators are used only inside your browser. They are not sent to or stored on our servers.

2. Information created or processed automatically

  • Browser storage (localStorage): the site stores small preferences, such as the last calculator tab you opened, in your own browser only. This is never sent to us and you can delete it in your browser settings.
  • Access logs: the site is served by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process access information such as IP addresses for security and operation.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics): we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. Information such as the pages you visit, time of visit, device and browser type, approximate location (country or city level) and how you arrived is sent to Google through cookies and similar technology, and we look at it only as aggregate statistics. The numbers and text you enter in the calculators are never sent. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Result share links: when you press “Share result link”, the values you entered are put after the # in the link’s address (URL). Browsers never send the part after # to the site’s server (GitHub Pages), so opening the link does not leave your values in server access logs. The link is created on your device only and the site does not store it. The messaging or email service you use and the person you send it to can still see the values, so send sensitive numbers such as salary, or a bank account you typed in, only to people you trust. Analytics receives only an address without your values. (Links made before October 18, 2026 carried the values after ?, which could appear in server logs; when opened now they are switched to the # form automatically.)
  • Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive technical information such as your IP address.

3. Cookies and advertising

To cover running costs, the site may use third-party advertising such as Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's advertising policies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some features may then not work as expected.

4. Sharing with third parties

Because the site does not collect personal information directly, it does not share any with third parties. The external services listed in sections 2 and 3 (hosting, analytics, fonts and advertising) process information under their own privacy policies.

5. Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to reply and handle your request, and delete them once that is done.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, email easycalc.kr@gmail.com.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update and its effective date on this page.
